
As a method of work, some principles from systems engineering will be used to serve as a framework to guide the process to find a solution concept. The main focus of systems engineering is to focus on the system as a whole. It is relevant to consider which interactions take place both with external systems as well as internally in the system that is in focus. Other external factors are the customer's (or user's) needs, interfaces, capabilities of the user and more which must be included in a system's design. One approach of systems engineering is the V-Modell which encompasses many steps from the definition of requirements to high-level design, detailed design and on the right side system verification and validation. These stages will also be used here as a reference. (Kossiakoff et al., 2020)
